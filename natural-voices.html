Google Natural Voices

Chrome comes with high-quality "Google (Natural)" voices that run on your computer. Chrome only turns them on after you open Reading mode, so Read Aloud can't enable them by itself.

Google Natural voices are not enabled yet.

To enable them

  1. Open Reading mode on this page: press Alt+Shift+R (⌥+⌘+R on Mac). You can also right-click anywhere on this page and choose Open in reading mode.
  2. Wait a few seconds while Chrome downloads the voices for your language. The status above updates by itself.
  3. When the status says the voices are enabled, you can close Reading mode and this tab.

To add other languages

In Reading mode, open the voice menu, choose Languages, and turn on the languages you want. Chrome downloads the voices for each language you turn on.

After restarting Chrome

Chrome turns the voices off again when it restarts. Downloaded languages are kept, so opening Reading mode once brings them back right away.
